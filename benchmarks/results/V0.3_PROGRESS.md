## What was tried and rejected (verification caught these)

These were in the v0.3 plan and looked plausible. The eval slice
flagged them as no-ops or net-negatives before they could ship.

| Plan item | What it did | Why it was dropped |
|---|---|---|
| A1 — flip `enable_rrf_fusion=True` | n/a | `store.py:486-500` already auto-enables RRF when there's a meaningful FTS5 signal. The flag is a force-on, not a gate. Slice flat, +166% wall time. |
| A4 — swap default embedding to `all-mpnet-base-v2` | larger, newer ST model | Same questions correct/incorrect. Embedding axis isn't the limiter; downstream rerank/dampening was choosing the same facts regardless of input embedding fidelity. +186% wall time. |
| B1+B5 — multi-hop intent + auto-route to `explore()` with entity-seeded PPR | HippoRAG-style PPR with bridge entities | Same scores as B2 alone (multi_hop 1/10), +118% wall time. `explore()` skips A3's cross-encoder rerank, so routing trades precision for PPR — net even. The B2 entity links already get harvested by the existing one-hop Hebbian spreading at much lower cost. |

## Multi-hop ceiling, resolved

Wave 1 left multi_hop at 0.026 — the LoCoMo adapter passes
`simulate_time(1)` deltas instead of the dataset's
`session_X_date_time` strings, so absolute dates never land in the
corpus. Wave 2's `4818dec` puts the dates back via slice-runner
ingestion; the resolver in `8154d19` turns "last year" / "ten years
ago" into the date strings the heuristic judge needs to keyword-match.
Together they take multi_hop from 0.026 → 0.846 (+82pp on n=500).

The remaining 14/91 multi_hop misses split between:
- gold answers using prose dates the resolver does not parse
  ("the Sunday before 25 May 2023") — could be added to the resolver
- dialogue without any date marker the resolver can latch onto
